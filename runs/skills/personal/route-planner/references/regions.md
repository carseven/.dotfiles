# Hazards and checks by region type

These are checklists, not facts about today. Verify each item with a current official source (park office, regional government, AEMET, club) and name the source in the route note. Mark the rest "unverified".

## Every region

- **Daylight:** sunset time on the date against start time + expected time + 1 h margin.
- **Weather window:** AEMET (Spain) or the local service. Plan the turn-back time.
- **Water:** sources on the route, and whether they flow in that season.
- **Exposure:** sac_scale T4 or higher, scrambles, via ferrata, glacier, snow slopes. This skill flags them. It does not plan them.
- **Access:** closures, permits, quotas, shuttle buses, locked gates, private land.
- **Phone coverage and emergency number (112 in Spain and the EU).** Tell someone the route and the return time.
- **Athlete limits:** injury rules from the plan, steep-descent share, total km-effort against recent weeks.

## Mediterranean ranges (Comunitat Valenciana, Catalonia, Murcia, Balearics)

- Summer heat. Start early. Carry more water than the map suggests. Many sources are dry.
- Fire-risk restrictions: regional governments can close forest access on high-risk days. Check the regional notice before a summer or autumn outing.
- Hunting season (typically autumn to winter, often weekends): check local signs and wear visible colours.
- Heavy autumn rain and flash floods in ravines (barrancs).
- Sharp limestone, loose stone and scrub on steep paths. Descents are hard on knees and ankles.
- Locked gates and farm access. Example: Wikiloc comments on the Les Creus track (Tavernes) report a locked gate near the tennis club around km 10.5 and a bypass.

## Pyrenees and other high ranges (Aragon, Catalonia, Navarre)

- Snow on north slopes into early summer. Check the snow state before routes above about 2,000 m.
- Afternoon thunderstorms in summer. Be below the ridge by early afternoon.
- Low temperatures and wind at altitude, even in July.
- National and natural park rules: quotas, bus access, no drones, dog rules. Example: Ordesa Wikiloc pages describe arriving at the Pradera by bus from Torla.
- Time per km-effort rises above about 2,000 m. Use a slower pace.
- Refuge opening and booking for multi-day or long routes.

## Atlantic mountains (Picos de Europa, Cantabrian range)

- Fog and rain are frequent. Navigation on limestone plateaus with sinkholes needs a track and a map.
- Access roads to popular points (for example the Lagos de Covadonga) can be limited in peak periods with bus service. Check the park notice.
- Long approaches. Distances were long for the climb: Lago Enol to Mirador de Ordiales was 9.9 km each way in the tested run.
- Livestock on pastures.

## Alps and similar (outside the tested regions)

- Hut seasons, snow and glaciers change the whole plan. Treat as high-risk: plan only marked hiking routes and send the user to local guides and hut wardens for anything else.
